# Lab 9 - Final Lab Challenge: Autonomous Troubleshooting

## 1. SCENARIO

Welcome to the final lab! Here, you will diagnose and fix a tricky connectivity issue between the **BU1 Frontend** and **BU1 DB**. As you may remember, in the previous lab you created several rules—in particular, your very last rule established traffic from BU1 Frontend to BU2 DB. However, that ICMP traffic is currently failing, which you can confirm by checking the Gatus dashboard on BU1 Frontend.

Unlike previous modules, this challenge provides `no walkthrough guide`. You are entirely on your own to isolate the root cause, verify routing and security policies, and restore full operational status.

**Sharpen your skills** using Aviatrix Copilot to inspect traffic flows, analyze topology maps, and accelerate your troubleshooting workflow. Good luck!
